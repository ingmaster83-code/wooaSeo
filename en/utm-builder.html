UTM Link Builder (Campaign URL Generator)

Add utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content and utm_id to links for ads, newsletters and social campaigns. Apply them to many URLs at once, normalise values to lower case with underscores, and get warnings about overwritten UTM parameters, query placement before the # fragment and mediums that do not match GA4 channel groups.

Example

✅ UTM added to 2 URL(s)

Input — Destination URLs (one per line - UTM is added to each)

https://example.com/pricing
https://example.com/blog/launch?ref=home#plans

Result — URLs with UTM

https://example.com/pricing?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=fall_sale_2026&utm_content=header_button
https://example.com/blog/launch?ref=home&utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=fall_sale_2026&utm_content=header_button#plans

Result — Applied parameters

Parameter | Value | Encoded
utm_source | newsletter | —
utm_medium | email | —
utm_campaign | fall_sale_2026 | —
utm_content | header_button | —

What this tool does

Attach UTM parameters correctly so GA4 can tell your marketing traffic apart. Your URLs are not uploaded.

  • Parameters: utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign (required), utm_term, utm_content, utm_id (optional)
  • Bulk: one URL per line; existing queries and the # fragment are kept
  • Cleanup: lower case with spaces → _ or -, or keep as typed; percent-encodes non-ASCII and special characters
  • Checks: missing required values, mediums GA4's default channel groups do not recognise, mixed case, duplicate existing utm_*, URLs over 2,000 characters
  • Tips: do not use UTM on internal links and agree a naming convention in your team

💡 Tips

  • Use mediums GA4 recognises (cpc, email, social, display, affiliate, referral) for accurate channel grouping.
  • GA is case-sensitive, so keep everything lower case to stop 'Email' and 'email' being counted separately.
  • UTM must be in the query string before the # so it reaches the server and analytics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do UTM parameters hurt SEO?

Search engines treat UTM URLs as the same page and consolidate them with canonical. Just do not use them on internal links, where they overwrite the traffic source.

What is utm_id?

In GA4 it links a campaign ID to imported cost data from an ad platform. Leave it empty if you have no such integration.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser; what you enter is never sent to or stored on a server, so you can safely paste data containing personal information, internal settings or API keys.
